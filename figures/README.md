# The cartogram family and its endpoint checks

`transport-rate.png` shows the approach to optimal transport on the plane and sphere. `clocks.png` shows why matching diffusion requires a change of clock, with downward triangles for errors below the display floor. Each has an SVG companion.

`world-bridge.mp4` shows the world illustration as a nearest-centre population colour field with mapped coastlines and graticule. `world-bridge.gif` is its 1000-pixel-wide transcode, playing inline in the [main README](../README.md) and linking to the MP4. `world-bridge-poster.png` shows the computed map at noise 0.002. The [world-map guide](../docs/world-map.md) records the method and limitations. `render-manifest.json` records inputs, encodings and output hashes for the renderer's MP4, poster and supporting figures; the GIF's separate transcode command is in [REPRODUCE.md](../REPRODUCE.md#render-the-included-world-maps).

[Reading the endpoint figures](../docs/figures.md) explains their marks and precision limits. [Rebuild commands](../REPRODUCE.md) use the included evidence and maps.
